# iPhone X vs. Xiaomi 15T Pro Comparison

Comparing the **iPhone X** (released in 2017) and the **Xiaomi 15T Pro** (released in late 2025) is a comparison between a classic flagship that redefined the modern smartphone and a cutting-edge, high-performance device from nearly a decade later.

## Key Specifications Comparison

| Feature | iPhone X (2017) | Xiaomi 15T Pro (2025) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display** | 5.8" Super Retina OLED (60Hz) | 6.83" AMOLED (144Hz) |
| **Resolution** | 2436 x 1125 (458 ppi) | 2772 x 1280 (447 ppi) |
| **Processor** | Apple A11 Bionic (10nm) | MediaTek Dimensity 9400+ (3nm) |
| **RAM** | 3 GB | 12 GB |
| **Storage** | 64GB / 256GB | 256GB / 512GB / 1TB |
| **Rear Camera** | Dual 12MP (Wide + Telephoto) | Triple 50MP (Wide + Telephoto + Ultrawide) |
| **Front Camera** | 7MP TrueDepth | 32MP |
| **Battery** | 2,716 mAh | 5,500 mAh |
| **Charging** | 15W Wired / Qi Wireless | 90W Wired / 50W Wireless |
| **OS** | iOS 11 (Upgradable to iOS 16) | Android 15 (HyperOS 2) |
| **Biometrics** | Face ID | In-display Fingerprint / Face Unlock |

## Major Differences

*   **Performance & Longevity:** The Xiaomi 15T Pro is significantly more powerful, featuring a 3nm chipset and 4x the RAM of the iPhone X. While the iPhone X is still functional for basic tasks, it no longer receives the latest iOS updates (capped at iOS 16), whereas the Xiaomi 15T Pro is at the start of its lifecycle with modern AI features.
*   **Display Technology:** The Xiaomi 15T Pro offers a much larger screen with a 144Hz refresh rate, making animations and gaming significantly smoother than the standard 60Hz on the iPhone X. It also reaches a much higher peak brightness (3200 nits vs 625 nits).
*   **Camera Capabilities:** The Xiaomi 15T Pro features a modern triple-camera system with much larger sensors and Leica optics, capable of 8K video and superior low-light performance. The iPhone X was a pioneer in portrait photography but lacks modern features like Night Mode or Ultra-wide lenses.
*   **Battery & Charging:** The Xiaomi 15T Pro has double the battery capacity and can charge to 100% in under 40 minutes. The iPhone X charges much more slowly and generally requires a battery replacement by now to maintain a full day of use.

## Summary
The **iPhone X** remains a beautifully built device and a piece of tech history, but the **Xiaomi 15T Pro** outperforms it in every technical category—display, speed, camera, and battery life—by a massive margin due to the eight-year gap in technology.
